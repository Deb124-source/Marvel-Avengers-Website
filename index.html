Avengers Heroes

Meet the legendary Marvel superheroes.

Captain America

Steve Rogers is the super-soldier known for bravery, leadership, and an unbreakable moral compass.

Iron Man

Tony Stark created the Iron Man armor and became one of the founding Avengers.

Thor

The God of Thunder from Asgard controls lightning and protects the Nine Realms.

Hulk

Bruce Banner transforms into Hulk, one of the strongest beings in the Marvel Universe.

Spider Man

Peter Parker fights crime using spider powers, agility, intelligence, and web-slinging abilities.

Black Panther

T’Challa protects Wakanda using vibranium technology and enhanced Black Panther abilities.

Doctor Strange

The Sorcerer Supreme protects reality using powerful mystical and multiversal magic.

Scarlet Witch

Wanda Maximoff uses chaos magic capable of reshaping reality itself.

Captain Marvel

Carol Danvers possesses cosmic powers including flight, energy blasts, and super strength.

Black Widow

Natasha Romanoff is an elite spy, assassin, and combat expert of the Avengers.

Hawkeye

Clint Barton is a master archer with unmatched precision and tactical skill.

Vision

Vision is an advanced synthezoid powered by the Mind Stone with incredible abilities.

Falcon

Sam Wilson fights using advanced wings and eventually becomes the new Captain America.

Winter Soldier

Bucky Barnes is a deadly super-soldier seeking redemption after his dark past.

Ant Man

Scott Lang uses shrinking technology while maintaining incredible strength and agility.

Wasp

Hope van Dyne fights with advanced shrinking technology and aerial combat abilities.

War Machine

James Rhodes pilots heavily armed armor built for high-powered military combat.

Loki

The God of Mischief uses illusion magic, intelligence, and manipulation skills.

Gamora

Gamora is one of the galaxy’s deadliest warriors and a Guardian of the Galaxy.

Star Lord

Peter Quill leads the Guardians with humor, music, and impressive combat abilities.

Rocket

Rocket is a genetically enhanced raccoon with engineering genius and weapon expertise.

Groot

Groot is a tree-like alien with regeneration powers and immense strength.

Drax

Drax is a powerful warrior known for immense strength and fearlessness.

Nebula

Nebula transformed from Thanos’ weapon into a fearless cosmic hero.

Mantis

Mantis is an empathic alien capable of sensing and influencing emotions.
